DiscussBuild is not just an idea — it’s real and built. It has a big future. It’s a bot you place on a repo, and it builds everything people discuss, every attempted pull request, everything. It builds per instance, on a fork, on a branch.

## The core idea

You place a bot on your repo and it builds everything people want to see built on your work. That’s good for them because they get to find out, and you get the possibility of a big merge.

Paired with staging customers — and those customers in the future could spend real money. If your AI project’s goal is to have everything built that people want built on your work, and that’s good for them because they discover it, and you get the possibility of that big merge — that could be a thing. It’s out there, it’s built, and use is made of these data centers in that we can potentially have staging.

## Staging customers

Staging is just getting started: the first piece is [staging-customers](https://github.com/fitzyracing1/staging-customers), which simulates customers of DiscussBuild on a test-money ledger. You could have staging customers where these bots — these customers are bots — decide whether or not to buy your product, your fork of a major product. They decide whether or not to buy it, and later potentially the author of that fork could get paid by these staging bots in money. These staging customers, which are bots, could in the future have money. I can build them, but building the ecosystem is going to take some time and needs advertisement.
